About the game

HUNT. GET PAID.
DON'T GET HUNTED.

Hunt the player in the lead, collect money crates, and build your loadout in the shop. Climb the standings and watch your back: soon the red arrow could be over your head.

01 / Multiplayer

HUNT THE BOUNTY.

Log in, host or join a room, and take on other bounty hunters. Every kill and every crate pushes you up the standings.

Open the game Online play needs the game server running.
02 / Quickplay

FIND YOUR FOOTING.

Get used to the movement, learn the corners of the warehouse and try out your loadout in offline practice.

Launch & choose Quickplay A little practice. A lot of firepower.
03 / The shop

SPEND THE BOUNTY.

Choose your guns, refill ammo, pick upgrades and spend your bounty on equipment. The shop also offers refunds if you change your mind.

What's new

CLASSIC BOXHEAD.
BUILT AGAIN.

The familiar game, rebuilt in HTML5 and JavaScript. Here's what the browser edition brings to the warehouse.

HTML5
+ JS

A new engine for a new era.

A native JavaScript game powered by Phaser, with HTML5 canvas and WebGL rendering. Play straight in a modern browser without Flash or Ruffle.

Browser native · No Flash player · No game install
01 / Performance

BUILT FOR SMOOTHER PLAY.

Rendering between game updates keeps movement smooth and makes higher frame rates possible. Your actual FPS depends on your device, browser and graphics settings.

02 / Graphics

THE DETAILS HIT HARDER.

Optional HD textures, sharper rendering, soft shadows, muzzle lighting and glowing tracers. New explosions add fireballs, smoke, sparks, debris and scorch marks. Switch between Enhanced and Classic graphics.

03 / Lighting

EVERY SHOT LIGHTS THE ROOM.

With Enhanced Graphics, muzzle flashes and explosions light up the walls, crates, cars and players around them. Soft contact shadows sit under every hunter, and floors darken where walls meet the ground.

04 / The interface

THE CLASSIC LOOK, POLISHED.

The original loading screen is back. The menus keep their colours and lettering, while the buttons gain crisp edges, a hover glow, a press and a clear disabled state.

The revamp is still growing. Turrets and additional game modes are future work; check Updates for the latest changes.
