The game you remember

HUNT. GET PAID.
DON'T GET HUNTED.

The player in the lead carries the bounty. Take them down to collect it, grab the money crates, and spend it in the shop before the next round. Then watch your back: now the red arrow is over your head.

01 / Multiplayer

HUNT THE BOUNTY.

Log in, host or join a room, and take on other bounty hunters. Every kill and every crate pushes you up the standings.

Open the game Online play needs the game server running.
02 / Quickplay

FIND YOUR FOOTING.

Get used to the movement, learn the corners of the warehouse and try out your loadout in offline practice.

Launch & choose Quickplay A little practice. A lot of firepower.
03 / The shop

SPEND THE BOUNTY.

Between rounds, the shop turns your money into firepower: new guns, ammo and upgrades, and refunds if you change your mind.
